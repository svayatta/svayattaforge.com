What sovereign means here

Three rules every tool in the forge is held to.

You run it

Self-hosted by default. Where there is a hosted option, it's a convenience, never a requirement.

You can read it

The full source is public under Apache 2.0, a licence that lets you use, modify, and ship it commercially.

You can check it

Where a tool makes a claim, it comes with a way to verify the claim that doesn't depend on us.

The tools

Each one is at a different stage, and each says so.

agent-custody

Proof of what your AI agents did.

A gateway between an agent and its tools. Every call is checked against a grant a person signed and a Cedar policy, then forwarded or denied, and a signed receipt is issued either way. Receipts go into a tamper-evident Merkle log, and anyone with the public keys can verify them in the browser.

TypeScript · Python · MCP · REST

Released on npm and PyPI, with a hosted log.

agent-custody.dev →Source

loom

Large MoE models on ordinary machines.

Runs Mixture-of-Experts models such as GLM, DeepSeek, Qwen, and Kimi across a group of machines. Compute stays local; what moves is weights: content-addressed byte ranges of a GGUF file, found through gossip and verified against a Merkle manifest before use. One static binary with an OpenAI-compatible API.

Zig · GGUF · Metal · Vulkan · P2P

Distributed weight sharing works across real machines. Untrusted peers is a design only.

Source →

tells

Is the other party on the call an AI?

Analyses a phone, Google Meet, or Zoom call as it streams and gives each party a score for how agent-like it is, from how it takes turns (reply latency, yielding, backchannels) and whether its voice sounds synthetic.

Python · streaming audio

Early MVP. The score isn't calibrated yet, and every output says so.
